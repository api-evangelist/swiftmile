# Swiftmile

> Swiftmile builds universal charging and parking infrastructure for micromobility — docking
> stations that power, secure and organize shared and privately owned e-bikes and e-scooters,
> including solar-powered units and integrated digital advertising panels. It is a hardware and IoT
> company, not a software vendor with a developer program.

hostname. Internet Archive snapshots show the real WordPress marketing site was live through at
least 2025-04-21 and had been replaced by the placeholder by 2025-07-09; it is still the
placeholder in the 2026-06-22 and 2026-07-18 snapshots.
